Set up a device for someone without an account

This makes a private invite link. When they open it on their phone or iPad, the app saves your key there so they can start reading right away. They use the same 5,000 reads a month as you, so send it only to someone you trust, by text or email.

How to get your free Bible key (about 5 minutes)
  1. On your phone or tablet, open api.bible and tap Sign up.
  2. Choose the free Starter plan and make an account with your own email. Check your email and confirm it.
  3. When it asks about your app, name it Bible Study and say it's personal, non-commercial use.
  4. If it asks which translations you want, include NASB.
  5. Copy your API key (a long code), come back here, and paste it into API.Bible key above.
  6. Tap Load versions on my plan to check that it works.

The free plan allows 5,000 chapters a month, far more than one person reads. For the ESV, get a free key the same way at api.esv.org.
