# Roadmap after the December 4 release

Melissa's plan for keeping the app growing without wearing the family out.

## Weekly (Saturday night, Central time) until the deep notes are finished
- Publish a batch of deeper study notes (more sections, words, history, scholar views with sources).
- A quiet "What's new this week" card shows once on next open. One tap closes it; Settings can turn it off. Never a forced tour.

## Monthly after that
- Refresh graphics with Claude's newest abilities.
- Navigation, interface and printing: friendlier on phones and tablets, still great on computers.
- Improve the kids' Bible and add headings.
- Looks: keep the family's favorites, retire looks nobody uses, and now and then add a new one or a seasonal one.
  Use a family-level count of which looks are in use (no individual tracking).
- Buddy: new emoji, icons and behaviors from time to time, so he feels like he's growing.

## Buddy as a companion
- He remembers small things each person teaches him (stored privately for that person).
- He grows warmer the more time someone spends with him, like a little pet or friend.
- Never annoying; the off switch and the "naps more" setting always stay.

## Standing rules
- No family reading plan, no tracking each other's reading, no notification reminders.
- Updates never lose anyone's place, notes or progress.
